# FDM design

Read the [engineering specification](../../../harness/ENGINEERING-SPEC-RU.md).
Use all intake/design checks in [the catalog](../../../harness/checks.json)
for every declared part and the assembly.
Design each product part for the finished assembly with `purpose: production`.
Use the specification's final-product criteria throughout; critical unknowns
block design instead of narrowing the task to a fit prototype.
Before choosing geometry or orientation, read the
[surface-quality procedure](../../../harness/SUPPORTED-SURFACES-RU.md).
Complete surface-quality-strategy and the per-part manufacturing_quality map:
functional/cosmetic faces, forming bases, actual acceptance criteria and the
free/functional-edge inventory. Apply the specification's correction workflow:
product function, fit, strength, quality and simplicity guide the choice.
Compare targeted native process settings and reasonable orientations of the
accepted geometry before a substantial CAD change. Avoiding supports is not
an acceptance criterion; neither flat nor on-edge printing is mandatory.
Qualify supported faces with a requirement/process/acceptance-budget engineering
basis or applicable physical results. Obtain new physical evidence only for a
remaining critical uncertainty. Justify actual edge sections by function; use
the three-road/four-layer fallback for thin features without a specific basis,
not as a universal quota for bevels or holes in bulk bodies.

Apply the specification's native-supports-only rule: printed model bodies in
CAD/exports represent functional product parts. Native nonprinting slicer control
objects are separate. Do not add temporary support bodies, fused sacrificial
geometry or CAD adhesion aids. At design stage plan the need and removal access;
actual supports are generated by the native slicer during preprint preparation.

1. Identify the run and require intake PASS before engineering changes. Retain
   authoritative CAD and measurements. Bind sources and assumptions; resolve
   critical provisional dimensions before design completion.
   Read the [component register](../../../harness/ASSEMBLY-COMPONENTS-RU.md),
   bind its accepted-baseline snapshot and record the component comparison.
   If redesign needs a component/fastener change, complete the specification's
   three necessity/alternative/independent reviews and obtain explicit user
   approval for the exact proposal before adopting it. Pending proposals remain
   separate from working geometry; a repair or trial label is not an exemption.
   Preserve simplicity and coherent load/assembly paths: no new spacers,
   adapters or washer stacks merely to keep old fasteners. Apply the mandatory
   assembly-simplicity check even when component_control says unchanged.
2. Inventory every loaded root, screw/insert/nut interface, fit, thin feature,
   bridge, overhang and support-removal risk. Assess assembly/installation forces
   as well as service loads. Apply catalog checks to every identified feature.
3. Develop geometry and orientation together. Measure actual root sections and
   material around holes. Generic wall limits and increased infill do not
   establish joint strength. Plan native slicer support detachment, extraction
   and tool access without building the supports into the model.
   For a substantial redesign record the actual cause, process/orientation
   alternatives and before/after effects on openings, mating geometry, loads,
   volume, appearance, assembly and printing. Show consequential choices early;
   informing the user is not a new approval requirement. Challenge analyzer or
   measurement assumptions before changing the product to satisfy a flag.
4. Rebuild exports and verify exact geometry, fits, intersections, fastener
   access and assembly sequence. Inspect assembly and print-orientation sections.
   Select a documented printability solution for every bridge and overhang.
   Verify architectural completeness too: disjoint valid bodies and aligned
   screws do not establish a complete enclosure. Use independent dimensional
   sources; coincident assumed coordinates do not validate a mating interface.
5. Bind current geometry/evidence. Record each check with actor, method,
   observation and file locator. Use [existing auditors](../../../harness/EXISTING-CHECKS-RU.md)
   only within their stated scope.
6. Run `python3 tools/engineering_harness.py check --run harness/runs/NAME.json --stage design`.
   Resolve FAIL/UNKNOWN and generate the checklist. Hand over the run and explicit
   physical validation limits to slicing.
   Reconcile final interfaces and quantities with the accepted register and
   approved delta. Update the register from evidence, preserving its history.

Changes invalidate affected checks. Diagnostic exports remain unreleased while
a stage is blocked. Continue authorized diagnosis and repair; summarize root
questions instead of treating dependent UNKNOWN rows as separate defects.
Group genuinely equivalent features with explicit coverage. Stop expanding the
analysis once the stated criteria are met. Do not describe diagnostic exports
as final or fit/load validated.
